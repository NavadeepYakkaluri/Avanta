Our Story

About Avood

Rooted in tradition, committed to purity — bringing you food the way nature intended.

Food Without Compromise

Avood was born from a simple belief: food should nourish, not harm. In a world flooded with processed snacks loaded with chemicals and preservatives, we set out to revive the age-old tradition of making wholesome treats from pure, natural ingredients.

Every laddu, bar, and brittle we create is handcrafted in small batches using recipes passed down through generations — sesame laddu sweetened with jaggery, nut brittles bound with raw honey, and energy bars packed with dates and seeds.

We never use artificial colors, flavors, preservatives, or chemical additives. What you see on the label is exactly what goes into your body — nothing more, nothing less.
